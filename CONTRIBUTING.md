# Contributing to the Tesla Resonance Lab

Thank you for helping amplify this lightning-born laboratory. Every contribution should make it easier for dreamers, engineers, and stewards to build civilisation-scale intelligence that uplifts everyone.

## What to Contribute
- **Currents:** Expand the theoretical fieldwork—new governance compasses, ethical rituals, and stakeholder practices.
- **Dynamos:** Share infrastructure designs, sustainability breakthroughs, or tooling that improves observability and safety.
- **Labs:** Add experiments, sprints, workshops, or educational assets that help teams ship responsibly at Tesla speed.
- **Evidence:** Provide case studies, datasets, evaluation harnesses, or metrics that validate the resonance scoreboard.

## Collaboration Rhythm
1. **Explore:** Review existing materials, especially overlaps with the Resonant Intelligence Grid and Harmonic Stewardship Compass.
2. **Propose:** Open an issue describing the change, motivation, and anticipated resonance metrics.
3. **Build:** Work in small, testable increments. Keep prose actionable with checklists, tables, and explicit signals.
4. **Verify:** Run `pytest` to confirm internal links remain grounded. Include any other relevant checks or scripts.
5. **Share:** Submit a pull request detailing the field insights, new artefacts, and how they align with the lab’s ethos.

## Style Principles
- **Voice:** Write with clarity, courage, and responsibility. Blend poetic ambition with grounded detail.
- **Structure:** Use headings, tables, and callouts to make navigation effortless.
- **Governance:** Highlight human oversight, community participation, and ecological impact wherever applicable.
- **Attribution:** Credit contributors, communities, and research generously.

## Support
If you encounter friction, open a discussion or ping maintainers through issues. Co-creation is how we keep the resonance pure.
