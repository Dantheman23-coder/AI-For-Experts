# Zero-to-Lightning Lab

A 28-day Tesla-grade invention ritual that transforms a raw idea into a resonant, production-grade pilot. Each day fuses scientific rigor, human empathy, and theatrical storytelling so the world feels the spark.

## Cycle Overview

| Week | Title | Objectives | Anchor Rituals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Signal Discovery** | Understand the people, processes, and energy flows you intend to transform. | Resonance interviews, field recordings, harm mapping |
| 2 | **Circuit Design** | Architect systems, guardrails, and data channels with clarity. | Frequency whiteboarding, safety dramaturgy, telemetry schematics |
| 3 | **Plasma Build** | Construct prototypes, evaluation harnesses, and stewardship dashboards. | Pair-programming relays, adversarial labs, governance rehearsals |
| 4 | **Lightning Launch** | Ship the pilot with human-in-the-loop, analytics, and communication artefacts ready. | Launch council, open demo night, aurora retrospective |

## Daily Flow

1. **Morning Charge (15 min):** Align on one transformative intent and the non-negotiable safeguards.
2. **Fieldwork Block (120 min):** Build, test, or research the highest-risk assumption alongside relevant stewards.
3. **Safety Sweep (30 min):** Run evaluation scripts, red-team prompts, and human override drills.
4. **Story Loom (45 min):** Update the narrative deck, visual dashboards, and user education assets.
5. **Evening Grounding (20 min):** Capture learnings, publish telemetry snapshots, and schedule follow-up actions.

## Artefact Suite

- **Resonance Map:** Composite of user journeys, energy flows, data sources, and policy obligations.
- **Capability Ledger:** Ranked backlog describing value, effort, risk, and readiness.
- **Harmonic Evaluator:** Collection of test cases, probes, and metrics spanning performance, fairness, safety, and sustainability.
- **Steward Console:** Live interface for interventions, rationales, and community feedback.
- **Launch Dossier:** Narrative memo, financial model, environmental impact summary, and governance attestation.

## Decision Gates

1. **Gate of Worth:** Do beneficiaries experience measurable uplift? Are externalities addressed?
2. **Gate of Safety:** Can the team demonstrate alignment evidence, override responsiveness, and recovery drills?
3. **Gate of Wonder:** Does the experience inspire adoption, trust, and advocacy?

## Collaboration Roles

- **Signal Keeper:** Synthesises insights, curates narratives, and ensures inclusivity.
- **Circuit Architect:** Designs system topology, guardrails, and telemetry.
- **Lightning Engineer:** Builds, tests, and optimises prototypes with sustainability in mind.
- **Steward Laureate:** Represents governance, ethics, and community voice.
- **Thunder Scribe:** Documents learnings, produces demos, and anchors retrospectives.

## Rhythm Enhancers

- **Nightly Coil Drops:** Short asynchronous updates summarising discoveries and unresolved puzzles.
- **Saturday Field Trips:** Immersive visits with affected communities or environments.
- **Sunday Resonance Reset:** Reflective session aligning goals, principles, and scoreboard targets for the coming week.

## Exit Criteria

- Pilot operates within Thunderstack Reactor using Aether Signal Mesh integration.
- Stewardship Compass metrics hit baseline thresholds for care, clarity, charge, and continuity.
- Community feedback indicates delight, trust, and willingness to co-create the next iteration.
- Energy and sustainability reports validate that impact outweighs cost.

The Zero-to-Lightning Lab proves moonshots can be responsible, regenerative, and electrifying all at once.
