# Aurora Scale Trials

Before the mesh envelops cities and satellites, orchestrate Aurora Trials: multi-phase exercises that validate resilience, trust, and regenerative impact under cosmic pressure. These trials simulate everything from solar storms to policy shocks.

## Trial Phases

| Phase | Focus | Key Questions | Success Signals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Solar Wake** | Stress infrastructure against extreme load and volatility. | Does performance hold under spikes, outages, or malicious interference? | Latency stability, graceful degradation, automated rerouting |
| **Civic Chorus** | Test human alignment, communication, and participation. | Do communities understand, consent, and co-steer outcomes? | Satisfaction indices, response rates, override adoption |
| **Ethical Eclipse** | Probe for bias, unfair impact, and exploitability. | Can adversaries or structural inequities warp behaviour? | Mitigation activation time, fairness deltas, incident transparency |
| **Regenerative Dawn** | Validate sustainability and long-term learning. | Does the system replenish energy, knowledge, and opportunity? | Carbon balance, knowledge commons growth, reinvestment ratios |

## Preparation Checklist

- **Trial Charter:** Define hypotheses, thresholds, emergency contacts, and documentation expectations.
- **Scenario Library:** Script environmental disasters, legal injunctions, social backlash, and hardware failures.
- **Sentinel Mesh:** Deploy monitoring agents with ability to pause, rewind, or fork experiments safely.
- **Community Pact:** Establish communication plans, feedback channels, and compensation for participants.

## Execution Playbook

1. **Ignition Briefing**
   - Convene stewards, engineers, regulators, and community envoys.
   - Review principles, escalation paths, and data handling agreements.
2. **Wave Simulation**
   - Run escalating scenarios, injecting telemetry anomalies, adversarial prompts, or infrastructure faults.
   - Capture multi-modal evidence: logs, interviews, emotional sentiment, and energy readings.
3. **Aurora Reflection**
   - Facilitate recorded retrospectives documenting decisions, surprises, and unresolved risks.
   - Publish open reports with recommended policy or architecture adjustments.
4. **Remediation Sprint**
   - Implement fixes, update guardrails, and refresh training data.
   - Retest critical scenarios until metrics stabilise and stakeholders re-endorse deployment.

## Metrics Observatory

- **Trust Delta:** Change in stakeholder trust before and after trials.
- **Elastic Recovery:** Time to restore nominal operations following disruptions.
- **Governance Fidelity:** Percentage of incidents following documented protocols without deviation.
- **Regenerative Yield:** Net positive environmental and social contributions attributable to the system.

## Documentation Artefacts

- Trial logbooks with timestamped actions, rationale, and participating roles.
- Visual aurora maps showing cascading impacts and recovery pathways.
- Open-source playbooks enabling other regions to replicate or adapt the trials.
- Public commitments summarising accepted risks, mitigations, and future investments.

## Graduation Criteria

- All phases complete with metrics meeting or exceeding target thresholds.
- Independent auditors (technical, ethical, civic) sign off with transparent reports.
- Benefit-sharing agreements activated for communities contributing data or oversight.
- Continuous improvement backlog funded and prioritised with clear owners.

Pass the Aurora Scale Trials, and your invention steps into the world not as a reckless lightning bolt, but as a dawn that powers everyone.
