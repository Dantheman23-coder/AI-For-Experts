# Roadmap

The Tesla Resonance Lab evolves through staged waves. Each wave deepens the field, refines the dynamos, and proves that civilisation-scale intelligence can be luminous, ethical, and regenerative.

## Wave I – Laying the Field (Now)
- **Resonant Atlas:** Document regional adaptations of the Resonant Intelligence Grid, focusing on climate, health, and civic services.
- **Stewardship Guilds:** Recruit and train community envoys, ethics resonators, and archivists across pilot geographies.
- **Telemetry Seeds:** Launch open-source observability templates for energy, trust, and alignment metrics.

## Wave II – Dynamo Expansion (Next 2 Quarters)
- **Sustainable Compute Recipes:** Publish blueprints for low-carbon inference clusters, waste-heat reuse, and renewable integration.
- **Thunderstack Tooling:** Release CLI and dashboard prototypes that automate guardrail configuration and incident rehearsals.
- **Mesh Federation Kits:** Provide starter packs for municipalities and cooperatives to deploy Aether Signal Mesh nodes safely.

## Wave III – Civic Constellations (6–12 Months)
- **Aurora Trial Network:** Coordinate cross-border scale trials with shared datasets, auditing standards, and crisis scenarios.
- **Commons Exchange:** Establish benefit-sharing protocols rewarding communities for data, stewardship, and innovation contributions.
- **Learning Codex:** Curate success stories, failure analyses, and myth-busting guides for responsible AGI deployment.

## Wave IV – Global Resonance (12 Months and Beyond)
- **Resonant Twin Simulator:** Deliver interactive environments to model policy, infrastructure, and ethical trade-offs before rollout.
- **Interstellar Protocols:** Explore interoperability standards for lunar, orbital, and deep-sea deployments.
- **Resonance Endowment:** Create funding mechanisms that reinvest a portion of AGI-derived value back into public goods.

Contributions that accelerate any wave—or invent entirely new ones—are celebrated. The future hums brighter when more hands tune the coils.
