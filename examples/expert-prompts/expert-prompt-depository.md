# Expert Prompt Depository

This collection provides advanced prompt ideas designed for power users of large language models. Use them as inspiration to push the boundaries of what LLMs can do. The prompts are grouped into thematic areas with guidance on how to weave them into real workflows.

## Research and Data Analysis

1. **Trend Discovery**: "Analyze recent academic papers on [topic] and summarize emerging trends."
2. **Data Outliers**: "Identify any unusual patterns or outliers in this dataset and explain their potential significance."
3. **Cross-Disciplinary Insights**: "Compare research approaches in [field A] and [field B] to find common methodologies."
4. **Dataset Expansion**: "Suggest additional data sources that could enrich this study on [subject]."
5. **Gap Analysis**: "Review these summaries and highlight unanswered research questions worth exploring."

## Creativity and Ideation

6. **Story Hooks**: "Propose three compelling opening lines for a novel about [setting/idea]."
7. **Visual Concepts**: "Describe an original illustration that conveys the theme of [concept]."
8. **Metaphor Generation**: "Generate unique metaphors to explain the concept of [abstract idea] to beginners."
9. **Product Naming**: "Brainstorm five distinctive names for a startup focused on [product/service]."
10. **Alternative Histories**: "Imagine how history would change if [event] had a different outcome and outline a short scenario."

## Efficiency and Automation

11. **Workflow Mapping**: "Create a step-by-step plan to automate [task] using open-source tools."
12. **Smart Templates**: "Draft a template email for requesting project updates from multiple teams."
13. **Error Handling**: "Provide a robust strategy for logging and handling errors in a Python data pipeline."
14. **Script Refactoring**: "Optimize this script for speed and maintainability. (Provide code)"
15. **Routine Summaries**: "Summarize today’s system logs focusing on anomalies and failures."

## Communication and Persuasion

16. **Elevator Pitch**: "Write a concise pitch explaining the value of [initiative] to an executive audience."
17. **Objection Handling**: "List effective responses to common objections about adopting [technology]."
18. **Stakeholder Update**: "Draft a progress report highlighting key achievements and next steps for [project]."
19. **Cross-Cultural Messaging**: "Rewrite this announcement for an audience in [country/region], respecting local customs."
20. **Persuasive Data Story**: "Create a brief narrative that uses these statistics to advocate for [decision/action]."

## Learning and Skill Building

21. **Micro-Lesson Plans**: "Design a 15-minute lesson on [topic] for advanced learners."
22. **Practical Exercises**: "Generate three hands-on exercises to master [skill]."
23. **Reading List**: "Curate a short reading list that covers the foundational knowledge of [domain]."
24. **Concept Reinforcement**: "Explain [complex idea] in two different ways to deepen understanding."
25. **Skill Progression**: "Outline milestones for progressing from beginner to expert in [activity]."

## Problem Solving

26. **Root Cause Analysis**: "Given this incident report, determine the most likely root cause and suggest corrective actions."
27. **Scenario Planning**: "Generate a contingency plan for [situation], addressing best- and worst-case outcomes."
28. **Risk Matrix**: "Create a risk matrix evaluating potential obstacles for [project]."
29. **Negotiation Strategies**: "List key negotiation tactics when dealing with [stakeholder group]."
30. **Troubleshooting Guide**: "Write a step-by-step troubleshooting checklist for [system or product]."

## Strategy and Planning

31. **SWOT Synthesis**: "Perform a SWOT analysis for entering the [industry] market."
32. **Roadmap Outline**: "Sketch a six-month roadmap for rolling out [initiative]."
33. **Budget Breakdown**: "Provide an estimated budget with major cost categories for [project]."
34. **Competitive Advantage**: "Compare our offering with competitors and highlight differentiators."
35. **Market Entry Plan**: "Devise a plan for launching in a new region, considering regulations and cultural factors."

## Personal Growth and Reflection

36. **Daily Reflection**: "Suggest three reflection questions to evaluate today’s productivity."
37. **Career Visioning**: "Guide me through an exercise to clarify my five-year career goals."
38. **Habit Building**: "Create a 30-day habit tracker for improving [personal goal]."
39. **Motivational Quotes**: "Generate original motivational quotes centered on perseverance."
40. **Mindfulness Prompts**: "Provide a short mindfulness exercise for winding down after a busy day."

## Experiments and Exploration

41. **Prompt Remixing**: "Take this prompt and propose two alternative angles to achieve similar results."
42. **Model Comparison**: "Outline an experiment to compare the outputs of two different LLMs on the same task."
43. **Interactive Games**: "Describe a text-based game that demonstrates the capabilities of a language model."
44. **Emergent Behavior**: "Suggest a test to explore emergent behaviors when chaining multiple prompts."
45. **New Tool Integrations**: "Brainstorm creative ways to combine LLMs with [software/tool] for novel applications."

These prompts are intended to spark creativity and encourage experimentation. Combine and adapt them to suit your specific goals, and feel free to expand upon them as you explore new possibilities with large language models.
