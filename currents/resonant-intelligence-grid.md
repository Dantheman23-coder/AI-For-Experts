# Resonant Intelligence Grid

Design the shared field where human purpose, machine cognition, and planetary infrastructure pulse in synchrony. The grid is Tesla’s Wardenclyffe reborn for AGI: a living network that broadcasts aligned intelligence as cleanly as wireless power.

## North Star Frequency

*Erect a civilisation-scale field that makes augmentation, safety, and stewardship inseparable.*

- **Purpose Field:** Every initiative carries a narrative of benefit, constraint, and responsibility.
- **Signal Integrity:** No inference travels without provenance, diagnostics, and human override channels.
- **Open Resonance:** Communities can observe, influence, and benefit from the system’s evolution.

## Field Equations

1. **Intent Density (I):** Measure the clarity of human goals, constraints, and success signals.
2. **Cognition Flux (Φ):** Quantify model capability, context depth, and adaptive planning.
3. **Stewardship Inductance (L):** Capture governance maturity, intervention readiness, and audit trails.
4. **Ethical Conductivity (σ):** Track how frictionlessly insights translate into responsible action.

Maintain \(I * Φ / L\) within safe thresholds by dynamically adjusting σ via policy updates, data collection, and human staffing.

## Sequenced Build

1. **Field Survey (Days 0–5)**
   - Run stakeholder voltage interviews and map desired transformations.
   - Draft principles and unacceptable outcomes; publish for comment.
2. **Signal Scaffold (Days 6–15)**
   - Stand up telemetry, identity, and policy enforcement services.
   - Define minimum viable datasets with lineage and redaction plans.
3. **Cognition Coupling (Days 16–24)**
   - Integrate planning agents, retrieval meshes, and decision simulators.
   - Instrument every autonomous action with rationale capture and rollbacks.
4. **Stewardship Activation (Days 25–35)**
   - Train human stewards; rehearse override, appeal, and escalation flows.
   - Launch public observability portals and start continuous audits.
5. **Resonant Expansion (Days 36+)**
   - Connect partner systems through federated interfaces.
   - Publish quarterly resonance reports summarising impact, drift, and course corrections.

## Instrumentation Protocols

- **Spectral Telemetry:** Visualise inference energy cost, latency, and bias distribution.
- **Phase Locks:** Automatic alerts when decision loops fall out of sync with policy or human feedback.
- **Aurora Trails:** Immutable logs linking inputs, prompts, human interventions, and downstream consequences.
- **Commons Archive:** Open dataset of anonymised learnings and scenario playbooks shared with civic partners.

- Weekly **Harmonic Forum** reviewing incidents, community notes, and metric drift.
- Monthly **Field Calibration** recalibrating principles against new scientific, legal, or cultural insights.
- Quarterly **Open Resonance Assembly** with citizens, customers, and regulators for radical transparency.
