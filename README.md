## Why This Lab Exists
Humanity finally has the compute and coordination Tesla imagined but never touched. We can braid global sensor grids, generative intelligences, and human stewardship into a single resonant field. This lab captures the schematics, rituals, and experiments required to summon that future responsibly.

We refuse incremental copilots. We ship coherent systems that:

- Illuminate hidden potential in energy, knowledge, and civic infrastructure.
- Harmonise autonomous agents with human judgement using transparent control loops.
- Scale without severing ethics, sustainability, or wonder.

## Quickstart

1. **Tune the field.** Align on purpose, stewardship, and non-negotiables using the [Resonant Intelligence Grid](./currents/resonant-intelligence-grid.md).
2. **Assemble the stack.** Wire planning engines, safety relays, and data coils with the [Thunderstack Reactor](./dynamos/thunderstack-reactor.md).
3. **Build the mesh.** Extend cognition to the edge devices and civic nodes through the [Aether Signal Mesh](./dynamos/aether-signal-mesh.md).
4. **Run the lightning sprint.** Prototype and launch with the [Zero-to-Lightning Lab](./labs/zero-to-lightning-lab.md) before validating scale with the [Aurora Scale Trials](./labs/aurora-scale-trials.md).
5. **Log the harmonics.** Feed telemetry, overrides, and community insight back into the stewardship compass.

## Design Ethos

1. **Frequency First:** Every system starts as a signal—measure resonance, interference, and amplification before code.
2. **Visible Currents:** Render decision flows, overrides, and energy draw in the open so humans steer with confidence.
3. **Wireless Humanity:** Connect talent, tools, and stakeholders as if telepresence were a native right.
4. **Regenerative Impact:** Intelligence must replenish communities, ecosystems, and knowledge commons.
5. **Mycelial Modularity:** Components should proliferate like living networks—adaptable, observable, and swappable.

## Resonance Scoreboard
Track the lab’s vitality across four harmonics:
